# Pit scouting

Pit scouting means visiting a team in the pits before matches start and asking about its robot.

1. Open the **Teams** tab. Teams that still need it show as **Not pit scouted**.
2. Swipe a team's row and tap **Pit Scout**, or open the team and tap **Pit Scout**.
3. Ask the team, don't guess. Good questions: What drivetrain? What can it score, and from where? What does it do in auto, and at the end of the match?
4. Add a photo of the robot if the team agrees.
5. Submit. Answers save as you go.

Be polite and quick: teams are busy fixing their robots. If they're in the middle of a repair, come back later.
