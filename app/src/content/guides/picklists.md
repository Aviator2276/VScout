# Building a picklist

A picklist is your ranking of the teams you'd want at alliance selection. Everyone can keep their own lists, and the team follows one.

1. Open **Scout → Picklists** and tap **New Picklist**.
2. Give it a **Name** and a **Purpose**: First pick, Second pick, Do not pick or Custom.
3. Tap **Add Teams**, search by number or name, and pick the teams you'd choose.
4. Order them best first. Drag a team by its rank number, or use **Move Up** and **Move Down**.
5. Tap **Add a Reason** under a team to write why it's there. Reasons help when the team compares lists.

## Other people's lists

Lists from other scouters are read-only. Tap **Copy to My Lists** to start from one and change it.

The **followed picklist** is the one an admin chose for the team (Settings → Admin → Event). It shows first for everyone. During alliance selection, teams that were already picked are struck through.
