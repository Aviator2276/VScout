# Building a picklist

A picklist is your ranking of the teams you'd want at alliance selection. Everyone can keep their own lists, and the team follows one.

1. Open **Scout → Picklists** and tap **New Picklist**.
2. Give it a **Name** and a **Purpose**: First pick, Second pick, Do not pick or Custom.
3. Tap **Add Teams**, search by number or name, and pick the teams you'd choose.
4. Order them best first. Drag a team by its rank number, or use **Move Up** and **Move Down**.
5. Tap **Add a Reason** under a team to write why it's there. Reasons help when the team compares lists.

## Other people's lists

Lists from other scouters are read-only. Tap **Copy to My Lists** to start from one and change it.

## Voting

Each person votes with one of their own lists for first pick and one for second pick. Your list's order is your ballot, so keep editing the list and your vote follows it.

1. Open your list, tap **⋯** and choose **Use as My Vote**. Or open **Picklists → Picklist Vote** and tap one of your lists under **Your Vote**.
2. The results rank every team by ranked choice. A team that more than half the ballots put first wins outright. Otherwise the team with the fewest votes drops out and those ballots move to their next choice, until one team has a majority. That team takes the top spot, and the vote runs again for the next one.
3. Teams on anyone's **Do not pick** list are flagged in red with how many people listed them. The flag doesn't change the order.

## The master list

An admin builds the **master picklist** from the results: tap **+** on a team to add it, or add every unflagged team at once, then reorder it like any list. The master lists show first for everyone, and the master first-pick list is the **followed picklist** the team uses during alliance selection, where teams that were already picked are struck through.
