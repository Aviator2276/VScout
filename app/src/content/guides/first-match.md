# Scouting your first match

1. Open **Scout** and tap **Scout a Match**. VScout suggests the next match and robot for you.
2. Check the team number on the robot's bumpers before the match starts.
3. Watch only your robot. Fill each stage as it happens: auto, then teleop, then the end of the match.
4. Something unusual? Add a note. If the robot stopped, mark it as disabled.
5. Tap **Review**, check your answers, then **Submit**.

Your entry is saved on your device right away and syncs when you're connected.
