# Scouting your first match

1. Open the **Scout** tab. Under **Needs Scouting**, VScout suggests the next robot that needs a scout. Tap **Start Scouting**. Want a different one? Tap **Other Options**.
2. Before the match starts, check the team number on the robot's bumpers. It should match the team at that driver station.
3. Watch only your robot. Fill each stage as it happens: pre-match, auto, teleop, endgame, then after the match. Tap **Next** to move on.
4. If the robot stopped, tipped or broke, log it under **Robot stopped or broke**: what happened and how long it lasted. A short brownout and a robot dead for the rest of the match are very different.
5. Write what you saw in **Notes**, not what you guess. "Pinned a blue robot against the wall for about 5 s" helps more than "played rough".
6. Tap **Review**, check your answers, then **Submit**.

Your answers save as you go, so you can leave and pick up again from **Resume** on the Scout tab. Your entry is saved on your device right away and syncs when you're connected.
