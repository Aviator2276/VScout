# Working offline

VScout saves everything on your device first, so you can scout with no signal in the stands.

- Your entries wait on the device until you're connected, then **sync** on their own. You don't need to do anything.
- **Scout → My Entries** shows each entry's state: drafts, pending (waiting to sync), synced, and any that need attention.
- If someone else changed the same thing while you were offline, VScout asks you which version to keep under **Settings → Sync Conflicts**.
- Don't sign out while entries are pending. Signing out deletes unsynced changes from the device, and VScout warns you first (**Stay and Sync**).

The schedule, teams and picklists download when you're online. Open VScout once on Wi-Fi before the event so they're on your device.
