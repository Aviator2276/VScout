# Watching a 2026 match

A 2026 match lasts 2:40. Here's what to watch for at each point, so you can fill the form as it happens.

## Before the match

- Check the team number on the bumpers. Note where the robot starts along its **starting line** and whether it's loaded with **fuel** (up to 8).

## Auto (first 20 s)

- Does it leave the starting line? Where does it get fuel: preload, **depot**, **outpost** or the **neutral zone**?
- Does it score in the **hub**? Does it cross a **bump** or go under the **trench**?
- Does it climb to **Level 1**? That's 15 points in auto.
- The alliance that scores more fuel in auto has its hub **inactive** first, in Shift 1.

## Teleop (2:20)

- Both hubs are active for the first 10 s (the **transition shift**). Then four 25 s **shifts** alternate which hub is active.
- Fuel in an **inactive** hub scores nothing. Watch what your robot does while its hub is off: collect fuel, feed partners, play **defense**, or wait?
- Robots may only shoot into their hub from their own **alliance zone**.
- Note defense in both directions: how well it defended, and how it handled being defended.

## End game (last 30 s)

- Both hubs are active again. When does it start climbing, and which **level** does it reach?
- **Tower protection**: touching an opponent robot that's on its own tower is a major foul.

## After the match

- Log fouls you saw clearly, like **pinning** for more than 3 seconds. Referees decide the real calls; you record what you saw.
- If the robot stopped, tipped or broke, say when and for how long.
